🏛️ About National Arts & Sports Club

Empowering youth, encouraging sports excellence, and building community unity since 1980.

Our History & Legacy

Founded in 1980 in Udma Padinhar, Kasaragod, National Arts & Sports Club has served as a cornerstone of youth development, sports promotion, cultural arts, and community welfare for over four decades.

Registered under Registration No. 75/2000 and affiliated to Nehru Yuvakendra (Reg No. NYKK-4912/KNG/630/2015-16), our club nurtures young talent in football, cricket, athletics, traditional arts, and charitable initiatives across Kasaragod and among our global diaspora.
